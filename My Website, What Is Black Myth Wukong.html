Black Myth Wukong, What it is based on

Black Myth: Wukong is a video game based on the Chinese story, 'Journey To The West.' Journey To The West tells the story of a monkey called Wukong who became immortal by breaking into the heavens and stealing ingrediants for an immortality potion. Buddha saw this and sealed him in a mountain for many many years where he was found by a monk called Tang Sanzang who asked him to collect scriptures from India.
