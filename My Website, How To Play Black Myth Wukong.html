How To Play Black Myth: Wukong
Black Myth: Wukong, sadly, is a PlayStation and PC exclusive game, so all you Xbox gamers will sadly not be able to play it. Black Myth can be played with a keyboard and mouse, but it is way way easier to play with controller. Due to its amazing graphics, the game can be pretty laggy. It has been optimised for PlayStations so unless you have a good PC, you may want to get it on your console. Before playing, navigate your way to the settings and look for the benchmark tool button (you may need to search up where it is). Use this to see what the performance will be like with your system and adjust till the fps counter is at around 40-60fps and it isnt too laggy. This will make your experience way better because you wont have to keep going into settings to adjust while playing. Now that you have done this you should be all ready to play! Start a new journey and enjoy the fantastic starting cutscene and your first battle! Good luck!
